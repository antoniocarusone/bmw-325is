Klaus — business card print sheet

This page is one US Letter sheet of 10 cards (standard 3.5 × 2 in, Avery 5371 / 8371 layout).
  • Print: Cmd/Ctrl + P → paper Letter, scale 100%, margins None (or Default), turn off "fit to page."
  • Paper: white cardstock (80–110 lb cover) looks best. Perforated business-card stock cuts itself; plain cardstock cuts on the dashed guides.
  • Before a big batch: print one, then scan the QR with your phone to confirm it opens buyklaus.netlify.app.
